Record a browser tab

Choose one tab in Chrome's picker. Other tabs, windows and system sound are excluded.

Ready. Nothing is captured until you choose a tab.

Download recording as WebM

Keep this recorder page open until you download. On stop, Chrome releases the selected tab and devices. Then use RecIt → Recordings → Import video. Imports stay local until you choose Upload to Loom.

Native RecIt zoom, noise filters, camera settings and hotkeys do not apply here. Long recordings use browser memory. Download before closing this page.
