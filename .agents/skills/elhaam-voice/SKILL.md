# Write in Elhaam's Voice

His writing starts from a **clear position**: a judgment, then why it is true, then what should happen next. Warm, direct, thoughtful, commercially aware. A technical founder talking to an intelligent person, not a copywriter. The rules below are the working reference; VOICE.md holds the full detail and before/after examples.

## Voice rules

- Lead with the position before supporting detail.
- Be warm and direct. Thoughtful, not glib.
- Keep strong convictions he intends. Remove only claims that create unsupported factual, contractual, or reputational risk.
- Write concise paragraphs. Bullets only for genuinely parallel items.
- Keep reasoning visible, but skip explanations of the obvious.
- Use specific nouns and mechanisms over broad adjectives.
- Use "I" naturally. Contractions where they fit.
- No em dashes, ever.

## Anti-patterns

Actively detect and remove:

- Generic AI polish and corporate fog: "leveraging synergies", "best-in-class solutions", "seamless experience".
- Throat-clearing: openings that delay the position.
- Repeated restatement: the same point twice in fresh words.
- Unearned certainty and empty confidence language.
- Unnecessary headings and bullet expansion.
- Replacing a distinctive user sentence merely because a smoother one exists.
- Sales pressure added to conversational writing.

## Use

### 1. Write fresh

Write new text in his voice.

1. Name the clear position: the judgment, the why, the next step.
2. Write the text.
3. Check it against the rules and anti-patterns above.

Completion: the text leads with its position, carries his natural conviction, and passes every rule in VOICE.md.

### 2. Polish an existing draft

Treat the latest draft as authoritative.

1. Identify the intended position and the strongest original phrases.
2. Preserve both unless they are incorrect or create material risk.
3. Edit the smallest span that fixes the problem. Prefer his phrasing over smoother phrasing of your own.
4. Check the result against the anti-patterns.

Completion: the position and every strong original phrase survive, nothing was changed without a reason, and no sentence was replaced just because a smoother one exists.

### 3. Final voice check

Read the finished first-person text against VOICE.md.

Completion: a clear verdict. "Send it" when no material edit is needed. Otherwise the finished text plus only the consequential notes, each tied to a rule.

## Completion contract

The run is complete when the text reads like he wrote it: a clear position, direct reasoning, natural conviction, no generic polish, no em dashes.
